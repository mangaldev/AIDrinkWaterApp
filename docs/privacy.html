Privacy policy

SipProof: Water Reminder · Last updated 18 October 2026

The short version

SipProof does not collect, store or share any personal data. Nothing you do in the app leaves your iPhone.

Camera

SipProof uses the front camera only while the drink check is on screen. Each video frame is analyzed on your iPhone with Apple's built-in Vision framework to see whether you are drinking. Frames are never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere, and the camera turns off as soon as the check closes.

Your drink history and settings

Your reminder schedule and your drink history (the time of each glass, and each skipped reminder) are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved.

Analytics, ads and tracking

SipProof contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code that tracks you.

Children

SipProof does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no data at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
