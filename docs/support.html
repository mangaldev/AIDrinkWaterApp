WaterSipAI: Water Reminder

How does the drink check work?

Hold your iPhone so your face is in view, bring your glass or bottle to your mouth, and take a sip. The screen shows five steps: face, mouth, cup, hand, and cup at your mouth. Each turns green once it's seen, and when all five are green a glass is logged.

The check doesn't recognize my glass

  • Use good light and keep the whole glass or bottle in the frame, not hidden behind your hand.
  • Hold the phone at arm's length so your face and the glass both fit.
  • Clear or very small containers are harder to recognize. A mug, a cup or a bottle with a label works best.

I can't drink right now

Tap Skip for now. It's logged as a missed glass in your Stats.

I'm not getting reminders

Check Settings → Notifications → WaterSipAI and make sure notifications are allowed. Then open WaterSipAI once so it can plan your next reminders.
